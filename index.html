WHO WE ARE

Türkiye's National
Petroleum Company

TPAO is Türkiye's national petroleum company, focused on the exploration, development and production of oil and natural gas resources.

Through innovation, advanced technology and a skilled workforce, we work to strengthen Türkiye's energy independence and contribute to the country's long-term energy security.
