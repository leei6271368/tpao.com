From geological exploration to drilling and production, TPAO works across the petroleum value chain to develop Türkiye's oil and natural gas resources.

WHAT WE DO

Energy Operations Built for the Future

Our operations bring together geological expertise, engineering capabilities, drilling technologies and production experience.

We operate in both onshore and offshore environments, developing energy resources through exploration, drilling and production activities.

01
01 — EXPLORATION

Discovering New Resources

Exploration is the starting point of our upstream activities. Our teams investigate geological formations and evaluate potential oil and natural gas resources.

Modern geological, geophysical and seismic technologies help us identify promising areas and improve our understanding of subsurface formations.

  • Geological studies
  • Seismic surveys
  • Offshore exploration
  • Resource evaluation
02
02 — DRILLING

Advanced Drilling Operations

Drilling transforms exploration opportunities into productive wells. Our drilling activities use specialized equipment, engineering expertise and advanced operational systems.

Our capabilities include both onshore and deepwater drilling operations.

  • Onshore drilling
  • Offshore drilling
  • Well construction
  • Drilling engineering
03
03 — PRODUCTION

Turning Resources Into Energy

Production is where discovered resources become part of the energy supply. Our teams operate and manage production activities with a focus on safety, efficiency and reliability.

We work to maximize the responsible development of domestic oil and natural gas resources.

  • Oil production
  • Natural gas production
  • Field development
  • Production optimization
OFFSHORE OPERATIONS

Türkiye's Energy Potential at Sea

Offshore exploration and drilling are an important part of Türkiye's energy development strategy. TPAO has developed capabilities for deepwater exploration and drilling in the Black Sea and Mediterranean.
