WHO WE ARE

A National Energy Company With a Vision

The Turkish Petroleum Corporation (TPAO) is Türkiye's national petroleum company, operating in the exploration, development and production of oil and natural gas resources.

Our mission is to contribute to Türkiye's energy security by developing domestic resources, investing in advanced technologies and building a strong and sustainable energy future.

01

Our Mission

To explore, develop and produce petroleum and natural gas resources efficiently and responsibly while contributing to Türkiye's energy security and economic development.

02

Our Vision

To become a leading energy company recognized for technological innovation, operational excellence and responsible development of energy resources.
